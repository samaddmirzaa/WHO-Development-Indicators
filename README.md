# WHO Development Indicators: Data Visualisation

Given a dataset of World Development Indicators spanning two decades and covering countries and regions worldwide, the goal is to find the stories worth telling and communicate them clearly through well-crafted visualisations.

The full brief and deliverable specification is in `WHO Brief.png`.

---

## Dataset

The dataset focuses on two key indicators tracked across countries and regions over a ~20-year period:

- **Life Expectancy**: average life expectancy at birth by country and region
- **Health Expenditure**: per capita health spending as a proportion of GDP

The data is sourced from publicly available World Bank and WHO development indicators and is open for educational and portfolio use.

---

## Analysis & Visualisations

The workbook (`WHO WorldDevelopment.xlsx`) explores the relationship between health spending and life expectancy outcomes, including:

- Global and regional trends in life expectancy over time
- How health expenditure correlates with life expectancy across income groups
- Country-level comparisons highlighting outliers — nations achieving strong outcomes relative to their spending, and vice versa
- Changes in the spending-outcome relationship across the two-decade window
